The operational proof points — in one demo.

SMS automation
Grace responds instantly to inbound texts in English and Spanish, classifies intent, and acts — no agent required.
🏥
Athena actions
Patient lookup, appointment lookup, confirm, cancel, reschedule, new booking, and case creation — all in the sandbox.

AI receptionist
Inbound voice with intent detection routes callers correctly without behaving like a traditional IVR.
🔁
Webex escalation
When humans are needed, Grace transfers with a full context pop-up — patient never has to repeat themselves.
Every interaction — SMS, voice, Athena result, Webex transfer — is logged to the dashboard with a transcript, summary, and audit trail.
